Commercial FAQ

Short answers before the first call.

How are learner licenses set?

The signed Customer Order Form records the number of learner licenses for the subscription period.

Can we start with a smaller group?

We can discuss the number of learner licenses you need. The agreed quantity will be in the signed Customer Order Form.

Is implementation required?

No. Implementation is optional and only provided where its scope is agreed in the signed Customer Order Form.

Are ongoing advisory services included?

No. The subscription does not include ongoing consulting or other services beyond any agreed implementation scope.

How long is the license term?

The signed Customer Order Form sets the start and end dates. The subscription does not renew automatically.

How is pricing quoted?

The signed Customer Order Form confirms currency, total price, applicable taxes, and any agreed implementation fee. One upfront invoice is payable within 30 days unless that form says otherwise.

Does WAIA replace legal or compliance advice?

No. WAIA supports practical Guidance and evidence from real work, but it isn’t legal advice, regulatory assurance, a GRC platform or a financial ROI calculator.

What happens after the first inquiry?

The first conversation is used to understand the current evidence gap, the number of learner licenses needed and the first evidence priority.
